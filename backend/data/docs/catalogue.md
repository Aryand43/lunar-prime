# Mission site catalogue
The site catalogue is a small, versioned list of historical crewed landing sites, robotic landing sites and proposed candidate landing regions, each with a source citation. It is separate from SPICE: the SPICE kernels used by this project contain Sun, Earth and Moon ephemerides and orientation, not a catalogue of lunar bases.

# Categories and what they do not imply
Crewed landing: historical Apollo lunar module positions. Robotic landing: historical robotic landers. Proposed region: a broad candidate landing region (for example NASA's Artemis III candidate regions); no precise point is plotted unless a sourced boundary or point exists. Planning waypoint: a generated, hypothetical point. User-defined: a position entered by the user. None of these is an operational base. Selecting or routing through a catalogue site does not imply logistics, infrastructure, power, supplies or communications.

# Coordinate sources
Apollo lunar module positions come from the NSSDCA Apollo Landing Sites page (LRO image measurements, Wagner et al. 2017). Robotic lander positions come from the NSSDCA Table of Anthropogenic Impacts and Spacecraft on the Moon. Both use the DE421 mean Earth/polar axis frame. The nine Artemis III candidate regions come from NASA release 24-136 (28 October 2024), which publishes no coordinates or boundaries.
